# Simple Form Builder Guide 📝

**For anyone - no technical knowledge required!**

---

## What This Does

Create custom forms that your team can fill out. Like Google Forms, but integrated into your system.

---

## How to Create a Form (3 Steps)

### Step 1: Name Your Form

1. Click **"Create New Form"** button
2. Type a name (e.g., "Customer Registration")
3. Optionally add a description
4. Click **"Next: Add Questions"**

**Shortcut**: Click **"Use Template"** to start with a pre-built moving form (you can edit it after!)

---

### Step 2: Add Your Questions

#### To Add a Question:

1. Click **"Add Question"** button
2. A dialog box opens with simple fields:

**"What question do you want to ask?"**

- Type your question exactly as you want users to see it
- Example: "What is your full name?"
- Example: "What is the moving date?"

**"Answer Type"** - Choose how users will answer:

- **Short Text** - One line answer (like name, city)
- **Long Text** - Paragraph (like special instructions)
- **Number** - Numbers only (like crew size, floor number)
- **Email Address** - Automatically checks if it's a valid email
- **Phone Number** - Automatically checks if it's a valid phone
- **Date** - Calendar picker
- **Dropdown** - User picks from a list you provide

**For Dropdowns:**

- In "Dropdown Options" field, type your options separated by commas
- Example: `Yes, No, Maybe`
- Example: `Residential, Commercial`

**Checkboxes:**

- ☑ **Required** - Check this if users MUST answer
- ☑ **Check if it's a valid email** - Only for Short Text (auto-validates)
- ☑ **Check if it's a valid phone** - Only for Short Text (auto-validates)

3. Click **"Add Question"** button (bottom of dialog)

#### To Change Order:

- Click the **up/down arrows** (⬆⬇) on the left side of any question
- Questions will appear in this order on the form

#### To Edit:

- Click the **pencil icon** (✏️) on any question
- Make your changes
- Click "Update"

#### To Delete:

- Click the **trash icon** (🗑️) on any question
- Confirm deletion

---

### Step 3: Preview & Save

1. Click **"Preview Form"** button
2. You'll see exactly how your form looks
3. Try filling it out to test
4. Click **"Save Form"** when satisfied
5. Done! You'll be taken to the form page where you or anyone can fill it out

## Example: Creating a "Moving Request" Form

### Step 1: Name

- Form Name: `Moving Request Form`
- Description: `For customers to request a moving service`

### Step 2: Questions

**Question 1:**

- What question: `What is your full name?`
- Answer Type: `Short Text`
- ☑ Required

**Question 2:**

- What question: `What is your phone number?`
- Answer Type: `Phone Number`
- ☑ Required

**Question 3:**

- What question: `What is your email?`
- Answer Type: `Email Address`

**Question 4:**

- What question: `What type of move is this?`
- Answer Type: `Dropdown`
- Options: `Residential, Commercial`
- ☑ Required

**Question 5:**

- What question: `What is your preferred moving date?`
- Answer Type: `Date`
- ☑ Required

**Question 6:**

- What question: `Current address`
- Answer Type: `Short Text`
- ☑ Required

**Question 7:**

- What question: `New address`
- Answer Type: `Short Text`
- ☑ Required

**Question 8:**

- What question: `Any special instructions or items?`
- Answer Type: `Long Text`

### Step 3: Preview & Save

- Review the form
- Test filling it
- Click "Save Form"
- ✅ Done!

## Tips

✅ **Start with a template** - Click "Use Template" for a head start

✅ **Question order matters** - Use arrows to reorder. Put important questions first!

✅ **Mark important as required** - Check the "Required" box

✅ **Dropdowns for choices** - Use dropdowns when there are only a few valid answers

✅ **Test before saving** - Always preview and test your form!

✅ **Edit anytime** - You can always go back and edit questions

---

## What Happens After You Save?

1. Your form gets a unique ID
2. You'll see it in the "Forms List"
3. Anyone can fill it out at the form page
4. All answers are validated automatically
5. Responses are saved (you can see them)

---

## Common Questions

**Q: Can I edit a form after saving?** A: Not directly, but you can create a new version based on it.

**Q: What if I make a mistake?** A: Just click the pencil icon to edit or trash to delete.

**Q: Do users need training to fill these forms?** A: No! They see simple questions and answer boxes.

**Q: Can I copy a form?** A: Yes - open a form and click "Use Template" when creating a new one.

**Q: How many questions can I add?** A: As many as you need! But shorter forms get filled out more.
